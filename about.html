Our Story

Olive & Thyme opened its doors in 2015 as a small family kitchen with only six tables. Our head chef grew up cooking with their grandmother on the Aegean coast and brought those traditional recipes to the city.

Today we are proud to serve hundreds of guests every week, but our idea is still the same: simple, honest food made with love. You can learn more about Mediterranean cuisine on Wikipedia.

How We Prepare Your Meal

  1. We buy fresh vegetables, fish and meat from local markets every morning.
  2. Our bread and desserts are baked in-house.
  3. Every dish is cooked to order – nothing is reheated.
  4. We serve it to your table with a smile.
